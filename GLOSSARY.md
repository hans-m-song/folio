# Folio

Folio holds transactions relevant to one business and prepares summaries for its
users. Personalization does not change the business represented by the repository.

## Language

**Financial account**:
A bank account or payment-card account associated with financial activity relevant
to the business. An association with a user does not establish tax deductibility.
_Avoid_: Account when it could mean a user login or an accounting category.

**Personalized summary**:
A view of the business's records relevant to the reviewing user, including
transactions directly attributed to that user.

**Financial year**:
The annual reporting period selected for tax preparation. In the current Australian
reporting convention, it runs from 1 July to 30 June of the following calendar year.
